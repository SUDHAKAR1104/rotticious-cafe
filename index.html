THE PHILOSOPHY

Beyond Roti, Kapda aur Makaan.

Generations were told that survival was about food, clothing, and shelter. But in modern Chennai, true living begins when the mind slows down, great coffee pours, and warm bread breaks.

Rotticious was born out of a rebellion against soulless commercial brews. We mapped out the highest-altitude estates in Chikmagalur to source shade-grown Arabica and craft-roasted beans. We installed stone-deck ovens to ferment slow Neapolitan pizzas, and created a sanctuary where remote workers, coastal dreamers, and dessert lovers unite.
